About Code-to-Flowchart Generator

🎯 Mission / Purpose Statement

Our mission is to instantly transform standard text code and pseudocode into clean, structured, multi-shape flowcharts featuring distinct terminals, inputs, decisions, processes, and outputs with customizable themes.

🛠️ Core Features / What We Offer

Supports automatic shape detection (`if/else` diamonds, `input/read` parallelograms, `print/output` parallelograms, `START/END` circles/pills, and rectangular processes), real-time Mermaid rendering, high-res PNG/SVG exports, and 1,000+ searchable algorithmic templates.

👥 Who It Is For

Designed for programmers, computer science students, teachers, and system architects who want clear diagrams without manual drawing tools.
